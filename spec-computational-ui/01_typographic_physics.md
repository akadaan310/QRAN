# Typographic Physics — Word Spacing, Depth-Shifting, and Lane Expansion

## Overview

Typography is the only telemetry surface the reader ever sees directly: every research quantity that would otherwise need a label instead moves, stretches, sharpens, or dims a letterform in real time. Weight encodes claim, tracking encodes tension, depth encodes distance from the current focus, and a rare shimmer encodes a structure that has just proven itself sound. None of these four channels shares a visual register with any other, so a reader can distinguish what kind of thing changed without ever being told in words.

## Weight as Claim

A lane's letterform weight rises continuously with its Rigidity Coefficient reading and falls just as continuously as that reading cools, easing rather than stepping between values so the eye reads it as breathing rather than flicker. The heaviest ink on the canvas at any moment marks whichever span is currently making the largest claim on the shared height budget described in `00_canvas_bounds.md`; the lightest ink marks spans making none. Weight is the slowest of the four channels to change and the only one visible even when the reader is touching nothing, so it functions as the canvas's resting state.

## Elastic Typographic Tension

Word-to-word and letter-to-letter spacing on a lane is driven directly by that lane's Rigidity Coefficient: as the coefficient rises, the physical distance between adjacent word-clusters stretches outward, exactly as a physical material stretches audibly and visibly under load before it would ever fail. This is not decorative kerning — it is the single visual readout of how much a line's grammar or attribution is currently under strain, and it is felt through the fingers as much as seen, since higher tension also lowers the touch-force threshold needed to trigger the seam-pressure responses in `03_tactile_interaction.md`. Low-coefficient spans sit close and calm; high-coefficient spans visibly labor to hold their shape, and the transition between the two states is always eased over a fixed short interval so a sudden coefficient jump never reads as a layout glitch.

## Dynamic Depth of Field

When an Attribution Vector's endpoint shifts — the discourse referent a passage is oriented toward changes mid-line or between adjacent lines — the canvas re-renders its depth axis rather than printing anything: the lane now carrying the vector's new endpoint scales upward and sharpens toward the reader, while every lane not participating in that vector softly desaturates and recedes half a step back, all within the fixed short interval. Depth changes are strictly relative and reversible — the moment the vector's endpoint moves on, the affected lanes ease back to neutral scale and full focus, and at most one lane holds forward position at a time so depth is never ambiguous about where the reader's attention is being pointed. A lane that has receded remains fully legible; recession lowers prominence, never contrast below the sacred legibility floor.

## Lane Expansion

Height and weight move together but are not the same channel: weight is a property of the glyphs themselves, height is the row's claim on shared vertical space, and a lane can be heavy without yet having won extra height if the equilibrium pass has not finished redistributing. Expansion always reads as the row *taking* space rather than space appearing around it — neighboring lanes visibly give ground in the same eased interval that the claiming lane grows, so growth and shrinkage are always one continuous motion, never two separate ones. A lane that reaches the sacred floor height stays at the floor no matter how much surplus deficit is still being redistributed elsewhere; the floor is enforced before the pass, not corrected after it.

## Invariant Harmonics

When a System Invariant closes cleanly across a long-distance jump — a structure that left the visible canvas and returned, or a relation that spans a distance most attribution does not survive, proves itself intact rather than broken — the letterforms at both ends of that closure emit one brief, subtle shimmer: a passing highlight across the stroke width, gone within the same fixed short interval the other three channels use, never repeating and never looping. The shimmer is the only channel that is momentary rather than sustained, because it marks an event — a closure completing — rather than a standing state like weight, tension, or depth. It never fires for a structure that merely holds; it fires once, at the instant balance is confirmed, and is not re-triggered by revisiting the same closure a second time without a new intervening change.
