# 15-Line Operational Spec Sheet — From Onboarding to Advanced Deep Tracking

A chronological path for one reader, from first touch to deep tracking. Times
are guided-session times; self-directed readers will vary, but the *order* of
unlocks is fixed — each stage assumes the habits of the previous one.

## Stage 1 — Arrival (0:00–0:10): the page as page

The reader opens the canvas and sees the 15-line page exactly as tradition
hands it down: calm, even, complete. Nothing moves. A brief guided tour
traces the geometry — 15 lanes, the gutters, the margin rails — with the
rails in their dim rest state. The reader learns the single most important
fact: **the page will never break**. The footprint is rigid; everything they
are about to do happens inside it.

*Unlocks:* lane focus (tap a line, it lifts slightly), margin rail reading
(the ticks and bars, read-only). *Mastery marker:* the reader can name all
three canvas zones — lanes, gutters, rails — and trusts the footprint.

## Stage 2 — First lens (0:10–0:25): between the lines

The guide invites a press-and-hold in a gutter. Light pressure: fine
filaments surface between two lines — a hidden connection made visible. The
reader tries firmer, then deep pressure, and watches the substrate change
speeds: connections, then skeletal underlay, then vertical root strands.
The gutter is no longer empty; it is a lens with three depths.

*Unlocks:* F19 at all three speeds, F18 margin scrub (depth cursor).
*Mastery marker:* the reader reaches for the gutter, not the text, when
curious about a relationship.

## Stage 3 — Perspective (0:25–0:40): the voice moves

The reader advances their focus down the page. On a shifting lane, the
canvas performs the full F14 sequence: tint warms, depth compresses, the row
splits into before/after voices around the seam. The Proximity Axis HUD is
introduced as the narrator — "watch the marker travel." The reader taps the
seam to merge the row back, then finds another shift on their own.

*Unlocks:* F14, F13 (axis HUD reading), F01 (rupture lens), F04 (shift lens).
*Mastery marker:* the reader anticipates a split from the axis marker's
movement before the row forks.

## Stage 4 — Substrate (0:40–0:60): the skeleton underneath

The pinch. Two fingers across the rows, inward — the markers dissolve row by
row from the epicenter, and the bare skeleton stands on gray. The guide says
nothing for a full minute; contemplation is the lesson. Then the spread:
markers heal back with their ripples, each landing exactly. The reader has
witnessed substrate independence as a physical event.

*Unlocks:* F17, F12 (rigidity rail — "feel where the page is hard").
*Mastery marker:* the reader describes the vocalization layer as "guests
that know their seats." Onboarding complete.

## Stage 5 — Intermediate (hours 2–10): trajectories and audits

- **Echo-trajectories (F15):** double-tap a root word, watch beacons rise on
  distant lines, enter orbit mode. The reader's first radial navigation —
  scroll feels impoverished afterward.
- **Friction literacy (F10):** reading the margin micro-bars, tapping to
  isolate hot spans, correlating weight on the lane with the number on the
  rail.
- **Collapse audits (F02 + F11):** long-press a lane into its key, open the
  audit, read superposition → survivors → loss. The reader starts asking what
  things cost.
- **Timeline checkpoints (F16, shallow):** crossing minor boundaries, feeling
  the detents, learning the rail's warning glow.

*Mastery marker:* the reader navigates by relation (orbit) more than by
position (scroll), and opens audits unprompted.

## Stage 6 — Advanced (10+ hours): locks, lenses, convergence

- **Path-trace locks (F16, deep):** the first deep chronological boundary.
  Forward motion dies against the glass; the reader reverses over the
  preceding lines, watches ghosts dissolve, recovers the anchor, feels the
  release. After several locks, boundary anticipation becomes a sense.
- **Lens stacking:** combining lenses — e.g., Rupture Lens over the Rasm
  canvas, or Convergence Lens inside orbit mode. The system permits defined
  combinations; undefined ones are refused with a clear rail signal, never
  silently.
- **Convergence overlays (F08):** pinch-testing distant lanes for footprint
  matches, reading the aligned ghosts in the gutter. The reader experiences
  the ledger's deepest finding firsthand: identical structure, zero channel.
- **Checkpoint authoring:** the reader places their own timeline checkpoints
  on the margin track — personal anchors for their own deep-tracking
  sessions. The track accepts them as first-class instruments.

*Mastery marker (deep tracking):* the reader moves through locks without
frustration, stacks lenses deliberately, authors checkpoints, and — the
final sign — begins to *feel* the checkpoint glow before it brightens. The
interface has become a sense, and the reader verifies structure the way a
musician hears intonation: continuously, and without being asked.
