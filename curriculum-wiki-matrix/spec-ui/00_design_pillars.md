# Master Core Design Pillars — The 15-Line Spatial Transformation

Four transformations anchor the entire proposal. Each takes one property of the
classic geometry and converts it from a static fact into a live behavior.

## Pillar 1 — Horizontal Row Dynamics (The 15-Vector Array)

Each of the 15 lines is treated as an independent horizontal vector lane with
its own live state. Typography is not locked into passive columns; lanes
respond to real-time perspective metrics drawn from the Discovery Ledger:

- **Expand / contract.** A lane's row height grows when its structural
  intensity is high (dense anomaly nodes, high friction) and recedes when the
  line is structurally quiet. The reader sees importance as vertical presence.
- **Slide.** A lane offsets horizontally in proportion to attribution-vector
  drift — when the line's relational links pull strongly toward distant
  regions of the array, the row visibly leans in that direction.
- **Depth.** Lanes carry a z-value from friction: high-friction lines lift
  toward the reader while quiet lines settle back; depth-of-field blur
  separates the planes.

Lanes never reorder. The 15-vector array preserves sequence absolutely; only
internal dimensions shift. This is what makes the transformation readable
rather than chaotic: position is sacred, dimension is fluid.

## Pillar 2 — The Margins as an Operating Track

The empty space flanking the 15 lines becomes an active instrument rail. Both
margins carry, per line:

- proximity indices (where the line sits on the address–speaker–absent axis),
- attribution vectors (the typed relational links touching the line),
- timeline checkpoints (chronological boundary markers the line crosses).

The margins are touch-active. Scrubbing a finger along the margin rail moves a
depth cursor across lines; tapping a margin instrument isolates its lane.
The margins never display text content — they display structure. Content stays
on the lines; measurement stays on the rails.

## Pillar 3 — The Inter-Row Substrate

The negative space *between* the 15 lines functions as a multi-speed lens
overlay. At rest it is simply space. Under interaction pressure it reveals,
in order of increasing pressure:

1. **Light pressure** — hidden connections: the relational threads linking the
   lines above and below surface as fine filaments.
2. **Firm pressure** — skeletal variants: the dotless structural skeleton of
   the neighboring lines shows through beneath the marked text.
3. **Deep pressure** — root continuity threads: the lexical roots running
   vertically through the page illuminate as continuous strands.

One gesture, three speeds, three depths of the same substrate. The lens never
obscures the lines; it reads *between* them.

## Pillar 4 — Rigid/Fluid Page Bound Equilibrium

The strict historical page-by-page footprint is preserved to protect visual
memory: 15 lines, fixed margins, fixed proportions, fixed alignment
boundaries. Within that rigid bound, the interior is fluid — row heights,
letter spacing, and depth shift to reflect text gravity and structural
intensity.

An equilibrium governor enforces the contract: total page height is constant,
so any lane's expansion is paid for by the contraction of quieter lanes. The
page breathes but never breaks. The reader always knows where they are; what
changes is how much space each line is allowed to claim.
