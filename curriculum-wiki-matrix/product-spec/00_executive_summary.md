# 00 — Executive Summary

> **Runway.** This document scopes a complete reader application that turns the fifteen-line page into a living structural instrument. It serves two readers at once: the novice who must feel oriented within sixty minutes, and the researcher who needs unmitigated depth without simplification. Everything downstream — pipelines, runtime, gestures, synchronization — exists to hold both readers on the same rigid page.

## Vision

A universally accessible reading environment built on the fifteen-line page geometry. The page keeps its historical footprint absolutely — fifteen lanes, fixed margins, fixed proportions — while its interior becomes an operating space: lanes breathe with structural intensity, gutters reveal hidden depth under pressure, and the margins carry live instrumentation. A first-time reader is guided; a researcher is never gated. Both look at the same page.

## Scope

**In scope:** the ingestion pipeline from three repositories to live lane-state values; the viewport runtime with its equilibrium governor; the full gesture dictionary and HUD state taxonomy; the three velocity tracks (Runway, Excavator, Collider); the synchronization schema for discoveries, checkpoints, and navigation state; the six-stage deployment plan.

**Out of scope:** interpretation or commentary on the text's meaning — the system reveals structure and verifies it; meaning remains the reader's work. Also out of scope: any alteration of the underlying repositories, any translation layer presented as primary, and any social or competitive mechanics (no streaks, no scores, no leaderboards).

## User personas

- **The Novice.** Arrives with no structural vocabulary. Needs the sixty-minute onboarding arc: page-as-page, first lens, first perspective shift, first skeleton reveal. Success is orientation and trust, not expertise.
- **The Visual/Physical Reader.** Learns through gesture and space. Navigates by orbit, feels boundaries through detents, remembers where things are by depth and position.
- **The Relational/Narrative Reader.** Learns through connection and story. Follows echo-trajectories as threads, experiences rollback as "going back," reads convergence as reunion.
- **The Systems/Logical Reader.** Learns through number and rule. Starts at the friction meter, opens audits unprompted, respects the equilibrium economy as a law.
- **The Deep Tracker.** The researcher persona all three profiles can grow into. Stacks lenses, authors checkpoints, anticipates boundary layers, verifies structure continuously. The system never simplifies for this reader and never hides its own mechanics from them.

## Product principles

1. The footprint is rigid; the interior is fluid. Visual memory is never sacrificed.
2. Content stays on the lanes; instrumentation stays on the rails. The separation is law.
3. Every compression shows its cost. Every merger shows its license. The system teaches suspicion of itself first.
4. Every gesture is reversible; every mode names its dismissal. No dead ends.
5. The Arabic letterforms are the primary visual material in every state. The interface language lives on the margins.

## What success looks like

A novice completes onboarding in under sixty minutes and can name the three canvas zones, perform the four signature gestures, and describe the skeleton as separate from its marks. A researcher runs multi-lens deep-tracking sessions, persists discoveries to the central schema without thinking about sync, and reports that the interface has become a sense. Both, when asked where they are, point to the same unbroken page.
